The Problem

Business reports are trusted — but rarely verified.

Quarterly summaries, investor updates, and team reports are full of numbers that look authoritative but are never checked against the raw data. Wrong date ranges, stale figures, rounding errors — they all slip through.

📅

Wrong time windows. A “monthly” comparison that actually spans 47 days overstates growth by design.

🔢

Cherry-picked figures. Reporting on the best-performing segment while ignoring the rest skews the picture.

📊

Silent calculation errors. A percentage rounded up by just 2–5 points leads to entirely different budget decisions.

How It Works

Four steps from report to evidence.

No spreadsheets. No manual checking. EvidenceLock does the math for you.

📊1
Upload Your Data

Drop in any CSV file — sales records, order data, financial exports. It becomes your ground truth.

📄2
Add Your Report

Paste the report text or upload a .txt file. EvidenceLock scans it for every checkable number claim.

⚖️3
Verify Claims

Each claim is re-calculated from the source CSV. The engine shows claimed vs. real and marks the verdict.

📋4
Get the Evidence

Download a corrected report with wrong numbers replaced by real ones, ready to share or present.

Why It Matters

Reading a report isn't the same as verifying it.

Every tool that summarises or visualises reports still trusts the numbers inside. EvidenceLock is the first step that doesn't — it re-does the math and shows its work.

🔁
Reproduces the Calculation

Rather than accepting numbers at face value, EvidenceLock re-runs the calculation from the raw data — the same way an auditor would.

🧾
Shows Its Work

Every verdict includes exact evidence: which rows were used, what values were compared, and why the verdict was CORRECT or WRONG.

📥
Delivers a Corrected Report

Wrong numbers are automatically replaced with the real calculated values and delivered as a downloadable corrected report.
